# Welcome to Permara

> **Trust endures. Value moves.**

Permara is building a decentralized protocol for verifiable payments, on-chain agreements, and funding. We're focused on making agreements executable—turning promises into reliable, traceable commitments that protect all parties involved.

## What We're Building

Permara focuses on four core pillars:

- **Verified Payments** — Transparent, auditable payment flows that build trust
- **Executable Agreements** — Smart, on-chain commitments that are binding and verifiable
- **Identity & Credentials** — Reliable identity verification for secure participation
- **Protected Funds & Trade Finance** — Safeguarded funding mechanisms for trade and commerce

## Our Mission

We believe trust is the foundation of commerce. In a world of decentralized finance, trust needs to be engineered—not assumed. Permara exists to make that engineering a reality, creating infrastructure where value can move with confidence.
