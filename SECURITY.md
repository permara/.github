# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

Permara is currently in early development. We do not yet have production releases.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our community and future platform seriously. If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in any of our public repositories or processes, please report it responsibly:

1. **Do not open a public issue** for security vulnerabilities.
2. Email your findings to: **security@permara.com**
3. Include a detailed description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce, and potential impact.
4. We aim to acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and provide updates as we investigate.

## Responsible Disclosure

- Please allow adequate time for us to address reported vulnerabilities before any public disclosure.
- We appreciate your cooperation in keeping our community and future users safe.

## General Security Practices

We encourage all contributors and users to:
- Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication on GitHub.
- Report suspicious activity on social channels (@permara_ on X).
- Review our [Terms of Service](https://www.permara.com/legal#terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.permara.com/legal#privacy).

> ⚠️ **Note**: Permara is building technology for verified payments and executable agreements. Nothing on this page represents deployed, live, or production-ready services. We are not making claims about guaranteed security or regulatory approval.
